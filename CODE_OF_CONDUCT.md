# Community conduct

We welcome contributions from people with different backgrounds and experience levels. Keep discussions respectful, specific and focused on evidence. Critique implementations and claims without attacking people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, publication of private information, and deliberate misrepresentation of test or hardware results are unacceptable. Respect licenses, hardware ownership and the authorization boundaries of robot deployments.

For community concerns, contact the repository owner through an available private channel. Do not post personal information in public issues. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions or restrict participation, with a proportionate explanation where appropriate.

Contributors and maintainers are both expected to follow these standards in project spaces.
